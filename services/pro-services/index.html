Common Questions

PRO Services — FAQs

What does PRO stand for and what does a PRO officer do?
PRO stands for Public Relations Officer. In the UAE, a PRO officer handles all interactions with government authorities on behalf of a company — visa applications, labour department submissions, municipality approvals, attestation and document processing. Most companies use an outsourced PRO service like ours rather than hiring a full-time PRO employee, which saves cost and ensures expert handling.
What government fees are included in the monthly package?
Government fees (medical test fees, Emirates ID fees, visa stamp fees) are charged at cost — we never mark them up. Our monthly package covers our service fee only. We provide a full breakdown of all government fees before any transaction so you have complete transparency on the total cost.
How quickly can you process an employment visa?
Typical employment visa processing in Dubai takes 5–10 working days from document submission to visa stamp, subject to ICA approval timelines. Medical test results take 1–3 days. We track every application and update you proactively — you never need to chase us for a status update.
Can you sponsor my family members if I have a free zone company?
Yes — UAE residence visa holders (both mainland and free zone) can sponsor immediate family members including spouse, children and parents, subject to meeting the minimum salary requirement (typically AED 3,000–4,000/month for spouse and children, AED 10,000/month to sponsor parents). We advise you on eligibility and handle the full sponsorship process.
What documents do I need to provide for a new employee visa?
For a new employment visa: passport copy, personal photo, educational certificate copy (some roles require attested), and a signed employment offer letter. For some professional categories (engineers, accountants, doctors) an attested degree may be required. We confirm the exact requirements when you contact us — requirements vary by nationality and job role.
Can I upgrade or change my package if my company grows?
Yes — packages are flexible and can be upgraded at any time with 30 days notice. If you consistently exceed your package limit, we proactively recommend an upgrade. There are no penalties for upgrading and the transition is seamless.
Do you handle attestation for all nationalities?
Yes — we handle document attestation for all nationalities. The process varies by country of origin (some require home country attestation before UAE MOFA) and we guide you through the full process. We work with all major embassies in the UAE and have established timelines for documents from all GCC, South Asian, European, African and American countries.
