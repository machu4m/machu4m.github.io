Liquidation

Closing Your UAE Company —
Done Correctly. Done Once.

Voluntary company liquidation in the UAE is a structured legal process. Done incorrectly, it leaves you liable for fines, visa blocks, and banking restrictions. We ensure every step is completed in the right order with every authority.

⚠️
Don't simply stop renewing your licence. Many business owners think they can just not renew — this results in accumulating government fines (AED 250–500/month), travel bans, and visa cancellation issues for all employees on the company's quota. Proper liquidation protects you.

The Liquidation Process — Step by Step

Mainland and free zone liquidation follow different processes. Below covers mainland (DED/DET) and most free zone liquidations.

1
Visa Cancellations — All Employees & Dependents
Before the company can be liquidated, all visas sponsored under the company must be cancelled — employees, investors, and family dependents. This includes Emirates ID deactivation and final exit or status change.
Cancel all employee employment visas and labour cards
Cancel family/dependent visas
Cancel investor/partner residence visas
Close MOHRE file and obtain labour clearance
⏱ 5–15 working days
2
Labour & MOHRE Clearance
Obtain a clearance certificate from the Ministry of Human Resources (MOHRE) confirming all employee obligations are settled — no outstanding gratuity, unpaid salary, or labour complaints.
Confirm all end-of-service gratuity paid
Check for any outstanding labour complaints
Obtain MOHRE no-objection letter
⏱ 3–7 working days after step 1
3
Tax & FTA Deregistration
If your company is VAT registered, you must deregister with the Federal Tax Authority before liquidation. Submit a final VAT return and obtain FTA deregistration confirmation.
File final VAT return
Submit FTA deregistration application
Obtain FTA clearance certificate
Corporate tax deregistration (if applicable)
⏱ 14–30 working days
4
Municipality & Utility Clearances
Obtain clearance certificates from the relevant municipality confirming no outstanding fees, and settle/transfer any utility accounts connected to the company.
Dubai Municipality clearance
DEWA / utility account closure
Ejari/tenancy contract termination
Signage permit cancellation
⏱ 5–10 working days
5
Bank Account Closure
All company bank accounts must be formally closed before or during the liquidation process. Obtain a bank closure letter for submission to DED / free zone.
Zero balance confirmation
Formal account closure request
Bank closure certificate
⏱ 5–21 working days (bank-dependent)
6
Liquidation Filing & Trade Licence Cancellation
Submit all clearances to DED / free zone authority. For mainland LLCs, a liquidation notice must be published in two UAE newspapers. The trade licence is then formally cancelled.
Appointment of liquidator (LLC requirement)
Newspaper publication of liquidation notice (2 newspapers)
Submission of all clearance certificates
Trade licence official cancellation
Commercial register deregistration
⏱ 14–21 working days after clearances
7
Final Confirmation & Archive
You receive the official licence cancellation certificate and closure documents. Keep these permanently — they are required if former employees apply for benefits or if any historical liability is ever questioned.
Official liquidation certificate
Certified copy of all clearance letters
Closed company document pack for your records
⏱ Company officially closed
