Terms & Conditions

Price Match Terms — Read Before Submitting

We want this to be fair for you and for us. These terms exist so there are no surprises either way.

✅ What qualifies
The competitor must be a UAE-licensed business setup consultancy — not a freelancer, informal agent or unregistered operator.
The quote must be written — on official letterhead, email, PDF invoice or formal proposal. WhatsApp messages, verbal quotes or social media DMs are not accepted.
The quote must be for the same service and same scope — same free zone authority, same visa count, same activity type.
The quote must include all fees — government fees, service charges and any mandatory extras. Quotes that exclude government fees are not comparable.
The quote must be current — issued within the last 30 days and not an expired promotional offer.
The quoted price must be lower than our published rate for the equivalent service.
❌ What does not qualify
Introductory or promotional rates that are not available to all clients — e.g. “first month free” or “launch offer.”
Quotes that exclude mandatory government fees, medical fees or Emirates ID costs that we include as standard.
Quotes from providers operating without a valid UAE business licence or professional licence.
Quotes for a different free zone, different mainland authority or different visa type to what you are requesting from us.
Services not listed in the eligible services section — including legal, court and complex bespoke packages.
More than one price match claim per client per 12-month period.
💰 Cashback conditions
AED 200 cashback is issued after full service completion — not at the time of booking.
Cashback is applied as a credit against your next service invoice, or paid as a bank transfer — your choice.
Cashback applies only when the price match claim is formally approved in writing by Emaar Global before the service commences.
Cashback cannot be combined with any other promotional offer, discount or referral credit.
If a service is cancelled before completion, cashback is forfeited.
📋 Our review process
We review all submissions within 24 business hours. Urgent cases submitted via WhatsApp are reviewed within 2 hours.
Emaar Global reserves the right to accept or decline any price match claim at its sole discretion. Decisions are final.
If we decline, we will explain exactly why in writing — not just “the claim does not qualify.”
We may contact the competing provider directly to verify the quote is genuine and currently available.
Submitting a false or altered quote is considered a breach of good faith and will disqualify the claim permanently.
Important: The Emaar Global Price Match Guarantee is a genuine commitment, not a marketing claim. We offer it because we are confident in our pricing. However, it is not an open-ended obligation — it applies to the services listed, under the conditions stated, for genuine like-for-like comparisons. We review every claim seriously and respond honestly whether we can match it or not.

For questions about eligibility before submitting: WhatsApp us first — we’ll tell you within the hour whether your situation qualifies.
