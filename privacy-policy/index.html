Privacy Policy

How Emaar Global Business Solutions collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information.

Last updated: July 2025 Effective: July 2025 Jurisdiction: United Arab Emirates
This Privacy Policy was last reviewed and updated in July 2025. If you have questions, contact us at info@emaar-global.com.
ℹ️
Summary in plain English: We collect only what we need to help you set up your business. We never sell your data. We never share it with third parties who aren't directly involved in delivering your service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.
1
Who We Are

Emaar Global Business Solutions LLC is a UAE-registered business setup consultancy operating under CBLS Licence No. 12491086. We help individuals and companies establish businesses in the UAE — including mainland company formation, free zone setup, visa services, Golden Visa applications, accounting, and corporate compliance.

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, Emaar Global Business Solutions is the data controller responsible for your personal information.

2
What Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you actively provide it to us — through our website forms, WhatsApp, email, or phone. We do not collect data passively beyond standard analytics.

Information you provide through our website forms:

✓
Full name
✓
Phone number and WhatsApp number
✓
Email address
✓
Business type and activity you are interested in
✓
Nationality and country of residence (where relevant to visa services)
✓
Any information you voluntarily include in message or notes fields

Information collected automatically when you visit our website:

✓
IP address and approximate location (country/city level only)
✓
Browser type, device type and operating system
✓
Pages visited and time spent on each page
✓
Referring website (how you found us)

Information collected during service delivery:

✓
Passport copies and Emirates ID (for visa and company setup applications)
✓
Documents required by UAE government authorities (e.g. NOC letters, tenancy contracts)
✓
Payment information (processed securely — we do not store card details)
3
How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

✓
Responding to your enquiry — to contact you about the service you asked about, answer your questions, and provide a quote.
✓
Delivering your service — to submit applications to UAE government authorities (DET, ICA, GDRFA, ADDED, free zone authorities) on your behalf.
✓
Communicating with you — to send updates on the progress of your application, request documents, and confirm completion.
✓
Compliance and record-keeping — to maintain records required under UAE commercial and anti-money-laundering regulations.
✓
Improving our service — to analyse which pages are visited most and where visitors come from, so we can improve our website and service offering.
✓
Sending relevant updates — where you have not objected, we may send you occasional updates about UAE regulatory changes (e.g. corporate tax, visa policy) that are directly relevant to your business. You can opt out at any time.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4
Who We Share Your Information With

We share your personal information only where it is strictly necessary to deliver your service or comply with legal obligations. We never sell your data.

✓
UAE government authorities — DET, ICA, GDRFA, ADDED, Ministry of Economy, free zone authorities, and other government bodies as required for your specific application.
✓
PRO service partners — freelance PRO agents engaged by us to attend government offices on your behalf. Bound by confidentiality.
✓
Accounting and legal partners — where you engage us for accounting, tax or legal services, relevant data may be shared with licensed accountants or legal professionals.
✓
Payment processors — for processing payments. We use established, PCI-DSS compliant payment providers. We never see or store your card details.
✓
Website hosting — our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (Microsoft). Standard server logs may include your IP address.

All third parties we share data with are required to handle it securely and only for the stated purpose.

5
How Long We Keep Your Data
✓
Enquiries that did not proceed to a service: 12 months from last contact, then deleted.
✓
Active client records: for the duration of service delivery plus 5 years, as required under UAE Commercial Companies Law and anti-money-laundering regulations.
✓
Government submission documents: 5 years from the date of submission, then securely deleted.
✓
Website analytics data: aggregated and anonymised — no individual records retained beyond 24 months.

You may request early deletion of your data at any time (subject to legal retention requirements — see Section 7).

6
How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable and appropriate steps to protect your personal information against loss, theft, and unauthorised access:

✓
Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our server.
✓
Documents submitted to us are stored in access-controlled cloud storage (not forwarded via personal email).
✓
Physical documents are handled only by authorised personnel and not retained beyond the period necessary.
✓
WhatsApp communications use end-to-end encryption by default.

No method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at info@emaar-global.com.

7
Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these, contact us at info@emaar-global.com. We will respond within 10 business days.

✓
Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
✓
Correction: You can ask us to correct any inaccurate information.
✓
Deletion: You can ask us to delete your data. We will do so unless we have a legal obligation to retain it (e.g. government-mandated record-keeping).
✓
Objection: You can object to us contacting you for marketing or update purposes. One message to us and we will stop immediately.
✓
Portability: You can request your data in a portable format.
✅
To exercise any right: Email info@emaar-global.com with the subject line "Data Request" and we will respond within 10 business days with no questions asked.
8
Cookies

Our website currently uses no tracking cookies and no third-party advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar tracking technology.

Standard browser cookies may be set by GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) for technical operation of the site. These do not track you for advertising purposes.

If we add analytics tools in the future, this section will be updated and a cookie notice will be displayed on first visit.

9
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services change or UAE data regulations are updated. When we do:

✓
The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change.
✓
If the changes are significant, we will notify active clients directly by email or WhatsApp.
✓
Continued use of our website or services after the update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
